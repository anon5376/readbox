A cleaner Readbox: a calmer look, two colour styles, and a few fixes.

**What's new**
- **Back to a clean Mac look:** a frosted sidebar, rounded buttons, and a quieter list with one title line and one detail line per article.
- **Two styles** under Settings → General → Style: **Graphite** (Apple greys and black, the default) and **Ink** (warm paper white with ink blue).
- **Every button now reacts when the pointer is over it.**
- **Gentler motion:** nothing bounces any more.
- **Simpler reading pill:** the pill at the top of the reader just shows how many minutes are left.

**Fixed**
- **Narrow, Medium and Wide now always look different.** Before, with large text or a smaller window, all three came out the same width. The Settings preview now shows the width too.

**Download**
- **Mac:** `Readbox_0.1.1_universal.dmg` (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later)
- **Windows:** `Readbox_0.1.1_x64-setup.exe`
- **Browser extension:** `Readbox-browser-extension.zip`. It hasn't changed since 0.1.0. Unzip it, then follow [the install steps](https://github.com/anon5376/readbox#browser-extension).

**Opening it the first time**
Readbox isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft developer certificate yet, so both systems warn you once:
- **Mac:** open the .dmg and drag Readbox into Applications. Open it; when macOS says it can't check it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen appears, click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Good to know**
This is early software, written mostly by AI coding agents and tested by a human. Expect rough edges, and please [report anything that breaks](https://github.com/anon5376/readbox/issues).
