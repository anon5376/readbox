Reading times that fit how fast you actually read.

**What's new**
- **Reading time learns your speed.** Estimates now start at 250 words a minute, about the typical adult reading speed. Each article you read moves them towards your own pace, and after 10 articles they use only yours. You'll see it in the list, the total at the top, the reader and the minutes-left pill.
- **Only real reading counts:** time spent scrolling with Readbox in front. Long pauses, time in other windows, quick looks and skims don't count.
- **Settings → Reading → Reading time** shows the speed Readbox has learned, with **Start Over** if you want it to learn again.

**Download**
- **Mac:** `Readbox_0.1.2_universal.dmg` (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later)
- **Windows:** `Readbox_0.1.2_x64-setup.exe`
- **Browser extension:** `Readbox-browser-extension.zip`. It hasn't changed since 0.1.0. Unzip it, then follow [the install steps](https://github.com/anon5376/readbox#browser-extension).

**Opening it the first time**
Readbox isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft developer certificate yet, so both systems warn you once:
- **Mac:** open the .dmg and drag Readbox into Applications. Open it; when macOS says it can't check it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen appears, click **More info → Run anyway**.

**Good to know**
This is early software, written mostly by AI coding agents and tested by a human. Expect rough edges, and please [report anything that breaks](https://github.com/anon5376/readbox/issues).
